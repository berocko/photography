This project builds on Camerapture's camera item and capture flow, image encoding and chunked transfer, world picture storage, photo rendering, picture frames, and physical album. Planned modifications add server-authoritative scene metadata, Gallery ownership and paging, appraisal/scoring and anti-farming rules, Expedition Credits providers, data-driven shops, configurable map-art conversion, stronger upload limits, persistence migrations, and dual-loader tests.

No code from the separately distributed Camera Mod is included or reverse engineered.
